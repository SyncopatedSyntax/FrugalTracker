FrugalTracker is an offline-first expense tracker that turns a few taps a day into real insight — trends, budgets, a savings rate, a spending heatmap — with every byte stored on your own device. No account. No server. No feed.

Capture

Log a spend in three thumb taps.

The add screen is a single thumb-zone flow — swipe forward as you go. A live budget ring at the top shows how the entry lands against your week, month and year before you even save it.

Every entry, searchable

An activity log you can actually mine.

Transactions group by day with running daily subtotals. Search across notes, categories and tags, or open the filter sheet to slice by type, category, tag and date range. Tags and notes ride along on every row, so “where did the money actually go” is a two-second answer.

Full-text search — notes, category names and #tags at once.
Calculator built in — split a bill or add tax right on the keypad.
Multi-line notes — and recent tags surface as you type.
Analyze

Four ways to read where your money goes.

Insights isn't one chart — it's four lenses over the same data, each answering a different question, and every one honours the period you're viewing: this week, this year, all time, or a custom range.

Overview

Net worth over time, plus a savings rate.

A running wealth line — with the previous period ghosted behind for comparison — and the headline numbers: income, expenses, cash flow, and the share of income you actually kept. The line's resolution scales with the span, so “All time” never collapses to a few flat points.

Breakdown

See exactly where it goes, in one glance.

A donut splits any period's spending (or income) by category, biggest slice first, with each category's running total and share right below. Prefer a different cut? Flip the same view to Labels and it's the identical donut, broken down by #tag instead — “#vacation” or “#side-hustle” across any period.

Categories

Tap a category, get its whole story.

Every category row expands inline into a 12-month trend, an average and total, and two honest deltas — versus last month and versus the same month last year — so lifestyle creep shows up as a number, not a hunch.

Calendar

A heatmap of your spending days.

Every day shaded by how much you spent, on a robust percentile scale so one big rent day doesn't wash the rest out. Zoom Month, Quarter or Year — then tap any day to see exactly what happened.

Budget

Set a limit you'll actually keep.

Most budgeting apps ask you to guess a number. FrugalTracker shows you the last six months of real spending — the moment you pick a category — and suggests a figure grounded in it.

The spending snapshot

Evidence, before you commit.

Pick a category and a mini bar chart appears: the last six complete months, a typical / average / high readout, and a trend versus the prior quarter. Type a limit and a dashed line drops onto the chart — months that would have blown it turn red in real time.

Read the bars — pink means that month went over the line you set.
Tap a suggestion — “Typical” or “Trim 10%” fills a round number for you.
Check the realism line — “covers 3 of your last 6 months” tells you if it's tight.
Overview & pace

Watch the year, not just the month.

The budgets overview sorts by used, left or over — over-budget rings surface first, and an “Over only” filter cuts to the ones that need attention. Open any budget for its year-to-date pace (are you ahead or behind for the year?) and a twelve-month spent-vs-limit history with over/under bars.

Cumulative, not resetting. A cheap month genuinely offsets an expensive one — the pace figure tells you whether you're up or down for the whole year, not just whether this month happened to be red.

Automate & own your data

Set the repeats. Keep the keys.

The recurring stuff logs itself, foreign spend keeps its real value forever, and everything you record is yours to take out whenever you like.

Recurring

Rent, salary and subscriptions on autopilot.

Define a rule once — daily to yearly, with an optional end date — and each entry is added automatically on its due date, never before. Missed occurrences backfill if the app sat unopened.

Multi-currency

A rate that's locked in for good.

Spend abroad and the exchange rate is captured at the moment you save. Update rates later and your history stays exactly as it happened — no silent rewriting of last summer's trip.

Own your data

Three doors out, always open.

Export a full re-importable JSON backup, a spreadsheet-friendly CSV, or sync to your own private GitHub repo. Restore replaces everything from a file; there's a Spendee CSV importer to get you in, too.

📲

Installs like an app

Add it to your home screen and it runs full-screen, fully offline — a real PWA, no store required.

🧪

Demo mode

One tap loads two years of realistic sample data (that's what you're seeing here). Your real data is safely tucked away and restored the moment you exit.

Personalize

Tuned to your hand, your eyes, your palette.

The defaults are just a starting point — colour, contrast and layout all bend to how you actually hold your phone.

Look & feel

Four palettes. Light, dark, or system.

Pick Sage Ledger, Botanical Bold, Coral Contrast or Ocean Punch — each its own full category palette — in light, dark, or matching your phone. A boldness slider then controls how strongly a category's colour shows through its round icon chip, from a whisper to fully saturated, live-previewed as you drag.

Built for your thumb

Move the whole keypad to one edge.

On a bigger phone, the amount keypad can hug the left or right edge instead of spanning full width — a shorter reach for one-handed use. Tap the gutter beside it to flip sides on the fly, or set a default in Settings.

Under the hood

Built to be fast, private and honest with your numbers.

A local-first single-page app with no backend to trust — the whole ledger lives in your browser's database.
